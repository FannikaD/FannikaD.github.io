The Pallet Guy

The Pallet Guy is an Auckland-based pallet collection business, working alongside Auckland Pallet Sales.

We specialise in collecting, reusing and recycling wooden and plastic pallets, helping businesses reduce waste and recover valuable warehouse space.

We offer customers a fast, reliable and friendly pickup and disposal service of pallets so that they can be reused as part of the recycling process, thus playing your part in helping the environment to reduce carbon emissions.


Benefits of pallet recycling

Decreasing Environmental Impact: Pallet recycling reduces the demand for new timber, helping conserve natural resources and protect forests.

Waste Reduction: By recycling pallets, we can significantly reduce the amount of wood waste and plastic waste sent to landfills, reducing landfill congestion.
