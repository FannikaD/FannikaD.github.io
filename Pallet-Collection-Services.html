How Collection works

When you have your pallets ready for pickup, reach out to us and we will organize a collection date at the earliest available date. If your collection is a reoccuring collection we will call you and sort out what date works best whether it is monthly, weekly, biweekly or anything else. Once the collection date is booked one of our team members will go to your site and collect the pallets.

We offer free collection when available however it is subject to location, quantity, condition and transport viability. Smaller quantities or predominantly damages pallets may attract a small removal fee.

Important things to keep in mind when booking a collection

  • We collect both wooden and plastic pallets
  • Regularly scheduled collection services are available upon request
  • One-off bulk pallet removal or warehouse clearances services are available
  • We do commercial and industrial collections, send us through any paperwork your site requires completed before the collection date
  • In order to be able to collect the pallets we require enough space for our truck to load up and a forklift to shift the pallets into the truck
